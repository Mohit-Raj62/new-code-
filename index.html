About Our Resort

10+ Years of Excellence

A Sanctuary of Peace & Luxury in the Heart of Nature

Nestled in the serene hills of Panchkot, Garh Panchkot Eco Tourism II is a premium eco-resort that offers the perfect blend of luxury and nature. Located in Rampur, Neturia, West Bengal, our resort is surrounded by lush green forests, ancient ruins, and breathtaking landscapes.

Whether you're seeking a peaceful retreat, an adventure-filled getaway, or a romantic escape, our resort provides world-class amenities while preserving the pristine beauty of nature. From our infinity pool overlooking the hills to our gourmet restaurant serving local and international cuisine, every moment here is designed to be unforgettable.
